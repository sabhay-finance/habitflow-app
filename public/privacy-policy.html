Privacy Policy for HabitFlow

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Summary: HabitFlow is a privacy-first application. All your habit data, streaks, and progress are stored exclusively on your device. We do not operate external tracking servers or collect personal data.

1. Information We Collect

HabitFlow does not collect, transmit, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties. All data generated by the user—including habit titles, schedules, completion logs, notes, and gamification milestones—is stored locally in your device's browser local storage or native sandbox.

2. Device Permissions

HabitFlow may request the following device permissions solely to provide core application features:

  • Notifications: Used solely to schedule local reminders on your device for scheduled habits at times chosen by you. No data is sent over the network.
  • Haptics / Vibration: Used to provide tactile micro-feedback when checking off habits or earning badges.

3. Third-Party Analytics and Tracking

HabitFlow does not include any third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, or external analytics frameworks.

4. Data Backup and Portability

Because your data resides on your device, you have full ownership and control. You can export your complete history into a standard JSON file or delete all stored data directly within the app settings at any time.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at: support@habitflow.app
